## Scientific role

**Reference only.** Projected-average-force methods may quantify free-energy barriers for activated defect motion or phase transformation. This is an advanced validation option rather than a near-term pipeline dependency, and the unresolved discovery link must first be mapped to an exact reviewed implementation.

## Possible ML usage

**Atomistic validation; data interpretation.** Any resulting quantity must carry the repository version, calculation method, inputs, units, temperature, alloy scope, and computational/experimental domain.

## Relevance to FeMnCoCrN HEA TRIP/TWIP project

With a validated FeMnCoCrN potential and a defensible reaction coordinate, barrier estimates could distinguish thermodynamic driving force from kinetic accessibility. This could refine the link between fault energetics/phase stability and whether loading produces TRIP or TWIP in the **SFE → phase stability → mechanism** chain.

## Limitations

Neither a potential nor a reaction coordinate is established. Barrier results can be sensitive to path definition, finite-size effects, temperature, sampling convergence, and local chemical/magnetic states. They cannot be inferred from a search link, transferred from another alloy, or used as condition-level mechanism labels.

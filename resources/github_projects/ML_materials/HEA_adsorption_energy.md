- **Possible relevance for FeMnCoCrN HEA TRIP/TWIP machine learning project:** May offer composition encodings or uncertainty practices, but surface adsorption is outside the deformation-mechanism target domain.
- **Priority:** Low

## Scientific role

**Not relevant.** Adsorption-energy ML concerns surfaces and adsorbates rather than bulk deformation mechanisms. Only generic representation or uncertainty-code patterns might be reusable; the resource has no direct scientific role in the current pipeline.

## Possible ML usage

**Feature engineering (method patterns only).** Any resulting quantity must carry the repository version, calculation method, inputs, units, temperature, alloy scope, and computational/experimental domain.

## Relevance to FeMnCoCrN HEA TRIP/TWIP project

A composition representation might be technically adaptable, but adsorption energy does not describe bulk stacking faults, FCC/HCP competition, or deformation kinetics. It therefore does not support **SFE → phase stability → TRIP/TWIP prediction** except as a distant software example.

## Limitations

Surface configurations, adsorbates, target energies, learned embeddings, and validation results cannot be transferred to bulk FeMnCoCrN. The entry is also only a discovery query. Adsorption-energy data must not be treated as SFE, Gibbs energy, phase stability, or mechanism evidence.

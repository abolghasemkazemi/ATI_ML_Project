## Scientific role

**Reference only.** This discovery entry may offer examples of HEA representation, benchmarking, uncertainty reporting, or grouped evaluation. It is methodologically informative, but it is not a validated FeMnCoCrN mechanism dataset.

## Possible ML usage

**Feature engineering; data interpretation.** Any resulting quantity must carry the repository version, calculation method, inputs, units, temperature, alloy scope, and computational/experimental domain.

## Relevance to FeMnCoCrN HEA TRIP/TWIP project

Composition encodings and evaluation practices may help test whether SFE and phase-stability descriptors add information beyond chemistry and processing in TRIP/TWIP prediction. Any benchmark design should preserve paper/material groups and should treat the causal chain **SFE → phase stability → TRIP/TWIP** as a hypothesis, not a guaranteed mapping.

## Limitations

An exact repository and dataset have not been resolved. Benchmark targets may be formation energy, phase class, or mechanical properties rather than deformation mechanism; chemical domains and sampling units may differ. Scores, pretrained models, splits, and labels cannot be transferred without target-semantic, provenance, independence, and domain checks.
